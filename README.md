# Ömer Kahyaoğlu

**Mathematical Engineering, İstanbul Technical University · Mathematics of AI**

I study the mathematics that makes learning systems work: estimation, low-rank structure,
optimal transport and the statistics of evaluation. I moved from Electronics and
Communication Engineering to Mathematical Engineering to understand *why* the algorithms
behind AI work. I am now in my final year, heading for an M.Sc. and a Ph.D. in the
mathematics of AI.
